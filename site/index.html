press, and hold. 按下，然后按住。

Forcify turns every press into one force value from 0 to 1. It reads real pressure from 3D Touch, Force Touch trackpads and pens, and uses a long press on everything else. Forcify 把每一次按压变成一个 0 到 1 的 force 值。3D Touch、Force Touch 触控板和压感笔会给出真实压力，其他设备上用长按代替。

Your device你的设备

Pressure hardware can't be detected in advance. An iPhone without 3D Touch reports the same force: 0 as one that is being touched lightly. So some flags only turn on the first time a real sample arrives. This is Forcify.detection in your browser, live. 压力硬件无法预先检测：没有 3D Touch 的 iPhone 和轻轻触摸的 3D Touch iPhone 报告的都是 force: 0。所以有些标记要等第一次收到真实数据时才会打开。下面是你的浏览器里实时的 Forcify.detection。

How it works原理

Each press uses the best source the device has, and every event says which one it came from. 每次按压都会用设备上最好的来源，每个事件都会注明来源。

sourcehardware硬件read from读取自

forcetouchMac Force Touch trackpadsMac Force Touch 触控板MouseEvent.webkitForce
penApple Pencil, Surface Pen, S Pen, WacomApple Pencil、Surface Pen、S Pen、WacomPointerEvent.pressure
longpresseverything else其他所有设备emulated: ramps 0 → 1 after LONG_PRESS_DELAY模拟：LONG_PRESS_DELAY 之后从 0 升到 1

events

A press, from start to end一次按压的全过程

A press emits forcestart, then force for every change, peek and pop as it crosses the thresholds, and forceend when it lets go. A pen above the element emits hover. 一次按压先发出 forcestart，每次变化发出 force，越过阈值时发出 peek 和 pop，松开时发出 forceend。悬停在元素上方的笔会发出 hover。

event

Every force event carries the value, where it came from, where the pointer is, how a pen is held, and how the press has gone so far. Try a pen to see the angles move. 每个 force 事件都带着数值、来源、指针位置、笔的姿态，以及这次按压到目前为止的情况。用笔试试，可以看到角度在变。

forcestart · peek · pop · forceend

Each fires at most once per press. A quick tap fires none of them. 每次按压中各自最多触发一次。快速轻点一个都不会触发。

          How long a press must be held, in milliseconds, before emulated force begins. Anything shorter is a tap. 按住多少毫秒之后开始模拟 force。更短的算作轻点。

            How far, in pixels, a pointer may move before a pending long press is cancelled, so a scroll or a swipe never turns into force. Press and drag at once: past the tolerance, nothing happens. 指针移动多少像素后取消尚未开始的长按，这样滚动和滑动永远不会变成 force。按下后马上拖动：超过容差就什么都不会发生。

                    HAPTICS: true

                    3D Touch made you feel each peek and pop. Forcify plays a light haptic on peek and a medium one on pop where it can: navigator.vibrate on Android, and on iOS 18+ the haptic Safari plays when a switch toggles. Both are best-effort. 3D Touch 让每次 peek 和 pop 都能被感觉到。Forcify 会在 peek 时播放轻触感、pop 时播放中等触感：Android 用 navigator.vibrate，iOS 18+ 借用 Safari 切换开关时的系统触感。两者都不保证一定有效。

                    false

                    PREVENT_CLICK: true

                    Once a press reaches peek, the click on release is swallowed, so a hard press doesn't also activate the button. Tap both, long-press both, and compare. 按压达到 peek 之后，松手时的点击会被吞掉，用力按不会同时触发按钮。两个都点一下，再都长按一下，比较计数。

                    DISABLE_NATIVE_GESTURES: true

                    A long press usually belongs to the platform: the iOS callout and link preview, text selection, dragging, Android's context menu, Look Up on a Mac. Forcify switches these off on its element. Long-press the right link on a phone to see what it prevents. 长按通常属于系统：iOS 的菜单和链接预览、文字选择、拖拽、Android 的上下文菜单、Mac 的查询。Forcify 会在它的元素上关掉这些。在手机上长按右边的链接，看看它阻止了什么。

                    CSS_VARIABLE: '--force'

                    Forcify writes the force to a custom property on the element, so styles can follow a press with no handler at all. Every pad on this page is drawn this way. Forcify 把 force 写进元素上的 CSS 自定义属性，样式可以直接跟随按压，不需要任何事件处理。本页所有按压区都是这样画的。
